How the number is made

Expected value = curve value × launch odds. The curve is fitted on this snapshot's own traded protocols, on every run — no hardcoded names, no outside prices.

Exclusion rule

Ranked: every protocol the snapshot records with no token, except exchanges, versioned pools of another protocol, and wrapped / staked / bridged products of a token that already trades (Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC categories; a name with a whole word wrapped, staked or staking; an exchange's name at the start). The snapshot carries no parent-protocol field, so nothing is excluded on a guess. A protocol the snapshot records with no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry — the board says “no token recorded”, never “no token”.

Verdict thresholds: FARM when even the low end of the 80% range clears $5M and gas stays under half the capital; THIN when the low end clears $1M; SKIP otherwise. The thresholds screen the protocol's whole expected value — they are not a promise of what one wallet earns.
